From folder to printer

Four steps, one app

Polydock does not replace your slicer. It handles everything that comes before: finding the right model and getting its colours ready.

  1. Organise

    Point it at your folder, NAS share or S3 bucket. Polydock groups the files into models without moving anything.

  2. Find

    Thumbnails, search, tags and collections. Every model opens in a 3D viewer.

  3. Paint

    Paint the model with your own filaments and keep several colour schemes for the same file.

  4. Send

    One click and the model opens in OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio or PrusaSlicer, paint included.

Library

Thousands of files, finally in order

Polydock reads your collection where it lives, groups it into models and keeps a thumbnail of each. Search by name, author, tag or file.

  • Nothing is moved or changed. Your folders stay as they are.
  • Collections and tags, with drag and drop.
  • Import ZIPs from Printables or MakerWorld as a single model.
Model page

Every model, in 3D and in detail

A viewer to turn the model around, its files sorted into variants (main, pre-supported…), its tags and its collections.

  • Variants: drag a file from one to another.
  • Technical details: size, triangles, filaments used.
  • Merge two models that belong together, or split one apart.
Send to slicer

From paint to print, no exporting

Polydock builds a painted 3MF and opens it straight in your slicer. Your scheme stays saved, so you can send it again three weeks later, exactly the same.

  • OrcaSlicer also gets your filament colours.
  • For AMS, MMU and tool changers, on any printer your slicer supports.
  • The original file is never changed.
On your machine

Questions

Is Polydock free?

Yes. The version you install yourself, desktop app or Docker, is free. Only the upcoming hosted service may be paid, to cover storage and servers.

Is it a slicer?

No. Polydock organises and prepares your models, then hands them to your usual slicer (OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio or PrusaSlicer), which makes the G-code. Polydock does not talk to your printer.

Does it change my files?

Never. Polydock reads your folders as they are. Paint, tags and collections are stored separately. With Docker you can even mount your folder read-only.

Which printers does it work with?

Any printer your slicer supports. For multi-colour you need a multi-material setup: Bambu Lab AMS, Prusa MMU, a tool changer or a multi-head printer.

Why do Windows or macOS show a warning?

The beta installers are not digitally signed yet. They do come from us: the install guide shows how to open them.

Where is the source code?

Polydock will be free software under the AGPL-3.0. The source code will be published with the public release; during the beta, only the installers and the Docker image are shipped.
